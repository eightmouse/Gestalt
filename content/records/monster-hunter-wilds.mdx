All that said, I don't know how much of the main story there is still but I'm loving every moment of it, can't wait for the endgame content though.

## Better than World?
I loved world, I only have 100-200 hours.. not a crazy amount since I picked it up late + I spent most of that in Iceborne and haven't fully enjoyed main story. However, I spent way more time in Rise on my Switch and then I bought it for Steam as well but haven't had the chance to play it properly.

World introduced a lot of QoLs that made the game definitely more friendly for people that never played a MH before, however I still felt like targetting hasn't improved much and mobility needed some improvements too, not super flashy and easy but something that would be much easier to pick up for outsiders of the genre.
I felt like Rise was a step in the right direction and they definitely improved all the little things that made World frustrating here and there.

Wilds is basically a further improvement, which is why it got lots of criticism too early on.. however I don't know how else to define that if not gatekeeping. Playing MH well is hard but I don't think the game should be TOO hard to pick up..
Outlanders on the other hand, I feel like is the sweet spot for casual and veterans. They introduced modern controls which are very welcomed and fit the mobile environment a lot.. should they decide to port them in future games... well I wouldn't mind them but I can already imagine the fuss it would cause.
