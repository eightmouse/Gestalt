# Deeper than expected
Bit of longer note today but a positive one. I'm about 20 hours in and the game keeps on delivering, time and time again. I know that a lot of the negative feedback were due to technical issues, however that didn't stop me from assuming the game, despite the performance issues and the bugs, was just not on par with World.

To be honest, I think World was simply an evolution and therefore it felt like an advancement, step ahead. Wilds surely has differences but it's nothing new, so obviously they're not on the same level of gameplay innovation. That said, I think the game is way better than it's said to be and past the technical annoyances that are still present, I think it's a good game.

On top of that, I thought the game was going to be shorter. This is also another complaint that I've heard a lot of and.. I think whoever complained exploded the game in one sitting or few days. I am of the opinion that games at launch should have at least 30-50hrs of gameplay, between main story and side quests + completion. At launch game was said to have 15-20 hours, it's quite lackluster YES.. at the same time tho, if you play 2 hours a day and you're enjoying the game:
- Not skipping videos
- Crafting all the different equips
- Experimenting with builds
- Trying all the different weapons
Game should've provided enough content for a little bit. Now, I might be wrong and maybe game had literally 0 things to do and the grand total would sum up to 20hrs. In that case, I understand the negative feedbacks and I'm on that side. HOWEVER, if the game had actually plenty of things to do but people were just rushing ahead doing 6+ hours sessions.. That is not the average.

# Where am I heading?

## Better than World?
I loved world, I only have 100-200 hours.. not a crazy amount since I picked it up late + I spent most of that in Iceborne and haven't fully enjoyed main story. However, I spent way more time in Rise on my Switch and then I bought it for Steam as well but haven't had the chance to play it properly.

World introduced a lot of QoLs that made the game definitely more friendly for people that never played a MH before, however I still felt like targetting hasn't improved much and mobility needed some improvements too, not super flashy and easy but something that would be much easier to pick up for outsiders of the genre.
I felt like Rise was a step in the right direction and they definitely improved all the little things that made World frustrating here and there.

Wilds is basically a further improvement, which is why it got lots of criticism too early on.. however I don't know how else to define that if not gatekeeping. Playing MH well is hard but I don't think the game should be TOO hard to pick up..
Outlanders on the other hand, I feel like is the sweet spot for casual and veterans. They introduced modern controls which are very welcomed and fit the mobile environment a lot.. should they decide to port them in future games... well I wouldn't mind them but I can already imagine the fuss it would cause.
