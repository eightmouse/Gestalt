# This is fun
I obviously don't grind 24/7 MMOs cause I would burn out and I'm grateful I have this game now, it's very fun and I like the feeling each character gives. I'm starting to understand builds and mechanics better, you can't just copy builds and quick buy forever.. at some point you have to see what item does what and for which situation.

I'm absolutely loving it, past the frustration for being noob or not being able to lane properly with random players.. I love it. Obviously the more you play these type of games the more sweaty and salty it gets heh.. however I've played tooooo much LoL and I'm older now, I don't really care that much anymore, I play my best and if I lose ohh well.

:::note 24 / 10 / 2026 - Pleasant surprise
Few days ago a friend kindly sent me an invitation for Deadlock! I was honestly not aware that it was invitation only as I didn't pay much attention on how to get the game. I've seen lots of positive feedbacks about the game but I've been away from competitive shooters and MOBAs for a while and, outside of few videos and people talking about the game, I didn't really check it out properly.

To my surprise, after playing for a little.. the game is quite fun ~
I was sceptical sort of, since I played my fair amount of this genre.. so I though "what else could be brought the table?" And I was proven wrong, luckily. Game is a mix of shooter and moba, you get the 3rd person shooting part with some mechanics from mobas. It's simple, lots of characters to play, straightforward. I like it.

Performance wise, despite being an alpha, it runs quite well and I haven't experienced technical issues,  for now..

I'm going to invite few other friends a probably play it more, I'm intrigued!
:::
