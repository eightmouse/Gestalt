Alright... where do I start?

I've been dealing with recurring stomach trouble for a while now, after a pretty rough bout of illness last year. It eased up for a time, then came back in waves and started getting in the way of normal life. Eventually I decided it was time to stop guessing and get properly checked out.

I've been working through it with my doctor over the past few months. Things are moving in the right direction, though recovery has been slower and less straightforward than I hoped. I'll spare the internet the medical report and treatment checklist; the short version is that I'm taking it one step at a time and trying to let my body do that too.

The physical side has been tiring, but the mental side has had its own weight. When something keeps returning, it's easy to pay attention to every little sensation and let worry fill in the blanks. I'm still working on not doing that so much.

That is a big part of why Gestalt went quiet for a while. I wanted to explain the gap without turning this into a medical file. I'm doing my best, and I hope to find my way back to writing here more regularly. For now, that's the signal.
