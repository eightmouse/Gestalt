:::note 04 / 11 / 2026 - PSA: Do not play NIKKE!
# Client Security Breach
A concerning security incident has emerged involving Goddess of Victory: NIKKE. According to independent security research, the game's official PC launcher was potentially used to distribute remote-access malware to unsuspecting players.

At the date of writing this note (November 4th), NIKKE's operations team acknowledged an anomaly suggesting that a third party may have tampered with the PC launcher's script files. The team reported deploying a client update and stated that it was still investigating the scope of the incident.

## What YOU should do
- Do not reopen the PC launcher until the publisher provides clear confirmation of remediation.
- If you used the launcher during the risk window, preserve relevant logs before deleting the game or cache.
- Check the launcher debug log for the reported malware indicator.
- Run updated security scans and inspect for the reported persistence mechanisms.
- If infection is indicated, disconnect the PC and seek thorough remediation; a clean OS reinstall is the higher-assurance option.
- From a separate clean device, change important passwords and revoke existing sessions.
- Rotate exposed developer keys or tokens, and secure cryptocurrency wallets where applicable.

A specific indicator to check
The research report identifies the following launcher log file:
`Launcher/intl_service/debug.log`
Within that file, search for:
`k2f_chan`

# Final thoughts
What makes this incident particularly worrying is the trust players place in official game launchers. Most people reasonably expect that downloading and running a game through its legitimate publisher won't expose their entire computer to an attacker.

This incident is also a reminder that security compromises can happen far beyond the obvious risks of unofficial downloads and modified clients.

As of November 4, the publisher has acknowledged the launcher issue and reported taking corrective action. However, the full impact, the number of victims and any confirmed data theft remain under investigation.

If you play NIKKE on PC, please take this seriously, check your system, and share the warning with other players.

# Intro
New entry and first note, yoo.
Like I mentioned in another note, somewhere in the archive.. this is a game I've been keeping on rotation, so it has been on my device for a while, never deleted it, YET.

I've downloaded Nikke about 2yrs ago almost, right when 'Mast: Romantic Maid' came out! I gave the game a try before, few times but it never clicked, till Mast.. for some reason.

## What is it?
It's an idle game, so you progress even when you're not playing since you stack on resources passively and the upgrade things. Obviously playing is important, you can't just afk.. advancing main story enhances the idle effect part, so you get more stuff when you don't play.

## Why is it so popular?
Big boobas, humongous cheeks and guns. I mean, gameplay wise game is good, I like seeing numbers on screen, big numbers... and making progress. But yeah, main catch are the waifus.. there are MANY and they're all different.
With 4th Anniversary, there are 2 new entries that are.. I don't have words to describe them.

These are just my 2 cents about the game. It has a lore of course and its quite good honestly, don't let the fanservice fool you.
